# Mbot2-DOUBLE-GRIPPER

A Mbot 2 compatible robotic arm capable of rotating via a servo and holding two objects simultaneously, with grippers that can change position to facilitate the transport and placement of the objects.

## Assembly Requirements / Hardware Needed
* **Compatible Platform:** mBot 2
* **Actuators:** Servomotors for rotation and the gripper mechanism, plus a dual-shaft DC gear motor (Yellow TT motor).
* **3D Printing:** It is recommended to print the parts with a 15% infill and a minimum of 4 walls (perimeters) to ensure the structural strength of the joints.
